🎮 Game Details
Welcome to this open-world sandbox prototype!
Explore the city, interact with civilians, and test various advanced mechanics including vehicles, combat, and procedural NPC spawning.

🕹️ How to Play
WASD — Move around

Spacebar — Jump

Shift — Run

1 — Toggle weapon (equip/unequip)

Left Click — Punch (if no weapon) or Shoot (if equipped)

E — Enter vehicles or doors

💬 Notes
You can interact with the environment by approaching doors and vehicles and pressing E.

Enemies and police will engage depending on your actions; stay alert!
